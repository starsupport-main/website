What we do

Help desk · Email · Networking · Security · POS · Websites

Most small businesses juggle three or four vendors — one for computers, another for the network, someone else for email, the POS and the website. We cover all of it, so you have one number to call.

Help Desk & Managed IT

Remote and on-site help when something breaks or slows down: computers, printers, logins, and the everyday calls. Ongoing support plans, with monitoring and patching. One number, one answer. Available 24/7/365, with standard hours 8 AM to 11 PM ET every day.

Microsoft 365, Google Workspace & Business Email

Professional email on your own domain, set up so mail lands in inboxes, not spam. Domains, DNS, and SSL, plus adding and removing users with clean access control.

Networking & Wi-Fi

Design, setup, and support for reliable, secure networks — switches, firewalls, VLAN segmentation, VPN access, and guest Wi-Fi. Fast, secure, and quiet, the way infrastructure should be.

Security Basics: Backups, MFA & Patching

Backups that actually restore, multi-factor authentication on email and key accounts, and computers kept patched and up to date. The basics that stop most small-business headaches before they start.

Hardware Supply & Setup

Sourcing, provisioning, and installing workstations, laptops, printers, Wi-Fi gear, POS terminals, and peripherals — spec'd for what you actually need and ready to run.

POS Support for Retail & Restaurants

Installation, programming, configuration, upgrades, and troubleshooting for Toast, Square, Clover, NCR Aloha, Lightspeed, Oracle Micros Simphony, plus retail platforms like Oracle Xstore and Aptos, and more. For retail counters and restaurants alike, from a single register to multiple locations and new store openings.

Websites
